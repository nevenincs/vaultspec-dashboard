# Add an aggregate and per-file diffstat to the proposal card, client-computed from the served proposal-detail bodies (the wire deliberately serves no diff), with terminal-right actions

## Scope

- `frontend/src/app/authoring/ReviewStation.tsx`

## Description

- Add aggregate and per-file plus-minus stats to the proposal card, client-computed from the served detail bodies through the same line-diff pair the diff panel uses, with actions terminal-right.
- Keep the card headless: the stat is a slot built by one shared helper all three hosts call, so the mounts cannot drift and the card still renders bare without a query provider.

## Outcome

Landed in 5e8a3d4f83. The first-pass attempt to embed the detail read inside the card broke fifteen of its own tests instantly - the headless fence held by test, and the landed slot shape preserves it. Review-confirmed across all three consumers.

## Notes

Two follow-ups carried into the closing batch: the truncation-floor honesty exists only as a data attribute and needs a visible affordance plus a test; the stat mounts the detail read per card (moved earlier, not doubled - cache serves the expansion), named as the first place to look if a full queue ever feels heavy.
