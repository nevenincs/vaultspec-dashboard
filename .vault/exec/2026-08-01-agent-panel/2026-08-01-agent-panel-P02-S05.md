# Adapt the orphaned vaultspec-planner persona to the vault Plan-document mandate and wire or delete vaultspec-reviewer and vaultspec-analyst in the same change

## Scope

- `src/vaultspec_a2a/team/presets/agents/`

## Description

- Rewrite the orphaned `vaultspec-planner` persona in place to the vault Plan-document authoring mandate: graph-submitter contract (emit, do not author), frontmatter and tagging rules, PLAN READY sentinel; flip `filesystem_write` to false per ADR D7.
- Delete `vaultspec-reviewer` and `vaultspec-analyst` persona TOMLs outright (pure deletions) and migrate their ad hoc test-fixture uses to a live agent.
- Add planner worker overlays to the adr-research team presets consistently with the existing adr-author overlay pattern.

## Outcome

Landed in commit 69f01764. Review-confirmed: zero residual references to the deleted TOMLs anywhere in the source tree; the remaining test-file hits are arbitrary string labels in pure routing-logic unit tests that load no TOML.

## Notes

Two lead-approved deviations. First, the role slug stays `planner` rather than the ADR prose's plan-author: the document-authoring contract and the deterministic acceptance provider resolve roles by suffix-matching the worker agent id, a tested invariant, and renaming would have churned ten unrelated fixtures for identical semantics. Second, DELETE was chosen over WIRE for the two orphans: wiring them would have required inventing a coder-lane team preset that ADR D7 explicitly fences out of this campaign.
